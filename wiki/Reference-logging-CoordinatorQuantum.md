# CoordinatorQuantum — implementation reference

**Classification:** Internal coordination primitive. **Owning header:** [src/logging/CoordinatorQuantum.hpp](https://github.com/ESPressio-Development-Platform/EDP-Logging/blob/feature/logging-v1/src/logging/CoordinatorQuantum.hpp). **Version:** 0.1.0, source branch feature/logging-v1. This page describes implementation and owner requirements; it does not replace normative ./docs contracts.

## Responsibility and boundaries

Runs one bounded coordinator unit of producer-queue polling and sink dispatch. It distinguishes bounded progress, quiescence and failure outcomes instead of implying that a source accepted into RAM is durable.

## Resource, failure and lifecycle invariants

Caller owns queue and sink lifetimes and supplies deadline/capacity. Only the owning coordinated worker may retire or reclaim record leases.

## Source-led complete status, outcome and bounded-execution contract (10 November 2026)

**Exact reviewed implementation:** [`CoordinatorQuantum.hpp`](https://github.com/ESPressio-Development-Platform/EDP-Logging/blob/8d2b307a8a1db1aa5596abc904cd0da1f3c032cd/src/logging/CoordinatorQuantum.hpp). The public enum `CoordinatorQuantumStatus : uint8_t` has six individually meaningful values: **QueueEmpty (0)** means no complete source claim was available; **BudgetConsumed (1)** means exactly the statically capped record quantum was processed and the caller must recheck stop and further work before sleeping; **ClaimFailed (2)** means the existing queue/claim owner could not establish a safe claim, not simply an empty queue; **ReclaimUnconfirmed (3)** means the last source lease could not be proven returned and must stay owned for reconciliation; **PriorClaimUnresolved (4)** means the caller passed a *still active* previous `ConsumerClaim`, which must not be silently discarded; **QueueContended (5)** means bounded queue-mutex transition encountered Busy/DeadlineElapsed before a source claim was made, and the queued source remains available. None is a persistent sink acknowledgement or ordinary application-level “everything delivered.”

The public `CoordinatorQuantumOutcome<TSinkCount>` is a fixed-size value record. **`TSinkCount`** is the statically selected 1–8 synchronous sink count; it determines exact array storage. `Status` defaults to QueueEmpty, **`Processed`** counts source records whose queue-claim lifecycle was processed, **`AcceptedByAll`** counts those successfully submitted to every callback and reclaimed, **`FailedSubmissionRecords`** counts records with one or more failed sink submissions or inability to reach fully submitted-and-reclaimed status, **`NotificationFailures`** counts confirmed native releases whose advisory capacity wake failed, **`PerSinkAccepted[TSinkCount]`** counts Accepted submissions by individual sink, and **`PerSinkUnaccepted[TSinkCount]`** counts their non-Accepted outcomes. These are independent finite per-quantum counters, not unique durable records across sinks or queued future completion.

The internal `Detail::DrainCoordinatorQuantumUnderQueuePolicy<TMaximumRecords,TBoundedQueueWait,TQueue,TSinks...>(queue,claim,maximumQueueLockWaitNanoseconds,sinks...)` reuses **existing** queue/Memory lease and synchronous copying sink callback owners. **`TMaximumRecords`** must be positive, defining a strict finite number of attempts; **`TBoundedQueueWait`** is a compile-time bool choosing the existing bounded queue-transition path rather than silently changing a default; **`TQueue`** supplies a lifetime-stable `ConsumerClaim`; **`TSinks...`** selects exactly 1–8 existing callback object types. The caller borrows all arguments for the entire synchronous quantum. A previously active claim causes immediate PriorClaimUnresolved; otherwise each `DrainOneRecordToSinksUnderQueuePolicy` can advance at most one record, updating per-sink and source counters. It detects Empty before incrementing processed; bounded mutex contention maps to QueueContended rather than ClaimFailed. It never retains a new callback copy, waits on a second Thread or allocates a dynamic queue.

The public `DrainCoordinatorQuantum<TMaximumRecords,TQueue,TSinks...>(queue,claim,sinks...)` is the legacy **NoWait** wrapper around that exact helper with `TBoundedQueueWait=false` and zero queue-mutex budget; it does not introduce a hidden infinite wait. The application-selected `DedicatedCoordinator` owns one stable claim, finite quantum budget, Threading wake/stop and sink service; it must reconcile any unconfirmed claim rather than destroy it. Callback blocking duration and physical Flash durability are **not** bounded or proved by this synchronous helper alone. GCC/Clang host tests and the named ESP32 managed-worker/strict-ACK profiles have separate finite receipts in [G1–G15 acceptance](../docs/ACCEPTANCE-G1-G15.MD).

## Responsibility and boundaries

Defines the scalar and semantic representations and success-aware reverse conversions required for the canonical LogRecord core fields, including certified fixed-width enum domains and existing ClockEra adapters.

## Resource, failure and lifecycle invariants

Conversion statuses are strongly typed rather than Boolean. Reject unknown or reserved enum/provenance values; never hide failed reverse conversion or imply that a native C++ scalar layout equals CBOR.
